Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 June 2026 · Updated: 30 October 2026 · Signature AI is developed by Essential Dev Team (Essential Dev Team).

We built Signature AI to be private by default. There are no accounts, no advertising, and no third-party analytics or tracking. The only statistics the app sends are optional, anonymous usage statistics to our own server, described below. This policy explains what limited data the app uses and why.

Information we use

  • The name and style you enter. When you generate an AI signature, the name and chosen style you type are sent to our secure backend (Supabase) and to Google's Gemini API solely to generate your signature image. This text is processed transiently to create your signature and is not stored on our servers afterward, nor used to identify you.
  • Signatures you create. Templates, AI signatures, and hand-drawn signatures are generated and stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud Sync in Settings, your saved signatures are also copied to your own private iCloud Drive (Apple), so they can be restored on your devices. We never receive or keep copies.
  • When you contact us. If you email us from the app (Support, Report a Bug, Request a Feature, Contact), we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply and fix problems. Report a Bug also attaches a technical log of what the app did (screens, errors as codes, iCloud and purchase status, credit counts) and any crash reports from Apple's MetricKit. The log never contains the name you type or your signatures, and nothing is sent until you tap Send in Mail.
  • Abuse protection. To stop automated misuse of AI generation, our server keeps, for each AI request, a one-way hash of your IP address (the IP itself is never stored) and a random app installation ID created by the app. They are used only to enforce daily limits, are not linked to you, and are deleted after 7 days.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We receive confirmation that a purchase succeeded so we can grant credits, but we never receive your payment-card or billing details.

Information we do NOT collect

  • No account or login is required, and the app never asks for your email address.
  • No advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics SDKs, no cross-app tracking.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.

Device permissions

  • Photos (optional). If you choose “Save to Photos”, the app saves your exported signature image to your photo library. Access is requested only when you use that feature.
  • Notifications (optional). If you allow notifications, the app sends a local notification when your AI signature finishes generating. These are generated on your device.

Third-party services

To generate AI signatures we use:

  • Supabase — hosts the secure function that relays your generation request. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • Google Gemini API — generates the signature image and style. See Google's API terms and privacy policy.
  • Apple — processes in-app purchases. See Apple's privacy policy.

Only the name and style text needed to render your signature is sent to these services, and only at the moment you tap Generate.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which features help people and to find crashes, Signature AI sends anonymous usage statistics to our own server (hosted on Supabase). This is our own first-party system — no third-party analytics or advertising SDK is involved. The app identifies itself only with a random installation ID that is created on your device and is deleted together with the app.

What is sent:

  • Device model, iOS version and app version
  • Language, region and time zone
  • Accessibility settings (such as larger text or reduced motion)
  • Session start and end, and the last screen you were on
  • App events, such as “a signature was exported as PNG” or “a signature was saved”, including the signature type (template, AI or hand-drawn) and the chosen style and ink — never the name or the signature itself
  • Purchase screen views and which credit pack was tapped
  • Purchase and restore events (the product only)
  • Crash and performance reports from Apple’s MetricKit

What is never sent: your name, email, phone number or address; your location; the advertising identifier (IDFA) or vendor identifier (IDFV); prices, receipts or transaction IDs; and anything you type or create — the name you enter, your signatures or drawings. Your IP address is not stored or used.

Retention: usage statistics are kept for 13 months (crash and performance diagnostics for 6 months), then deleted.

Turn it off: Settings → Privacy → Share Usage Data. When off, nothing is collected or sent, and anything not yet sent is deleted from your device.

Delete it: copy your Installation ID from Settings → Privacy and email it to essentialdevteam@gmail.com; we will delete everything linked to it.

Children's privacy

Signature AI is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention

We do not retain the names or signatures you create on our servers. Anything stored in the app's gallery lives on your device (and in your own iCloud Drive if iCloud Sync is on) and is removed when you delete it. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to help you.

Your rights

Because we don't maintain accounts or store your content, there is no personal profile to access or delete on our side. You can remove all in-app content at any time by deleting items in the app or uninstalling it. To delete the anonymous usage statistics linked to your installation, email us your Installation ID (Settings → Privacy) as described above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new effective date.
